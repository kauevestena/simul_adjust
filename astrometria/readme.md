The idea of this simulator is to simulate the positioning of the viewport, by using astronomical observations.

I'm thinking that maybe Cesium would be a good fit for this, but I'm open to other suggestions.

The idea is, you start at a random position in the world, and you need to observe stars to find your position.

The procedures shall happen as if you were a surveyor of the last century, without GNSS

You will have a theodolite, and you will need to observe stars.

The viewpor is the sky as seen throu the theodolite lens, at your side you will see zenith distance reading and the direction reading (arbitrary at the begining)

Maybe when you turn around the star data can show up (maybe like a tooltip), with the data of the star (name, RA, DEC, right ascension, declination, etc.) - It will be like having a star catalogue in front of you, with the star in the centre of the view

In the screen the Greenwich time will appear, similar on how it was get (by radio broadcast)
